<h3 class="pub-title">Enhancing mixed emotion recognition through XAI-enabled deep learning on multimodal dataset</h3>
<div class="pub-authors"><span class="me">Anwar, A. R.</span>, Zhao, Y., Hoog Antink, C., &amp; Güney, G.</div>

<details class="pub-abstract">
<summary>Abstract</summary>
<p>Accurate recognition of mixed emotions, where individuals simultaneously experience multiple emotional states, has emerged as a crucial area of focus in affective computing research. Identifying these complex, composite emotions is essential for developing more robust and human-like emotion recognition systems. This study proposes the development of a new mixed emotion recognition (MER) pipeline that leverages the multimodal dataset consisting of four types of data, namely Electroencephalography, Galvanic Skin Response, Photoplethysmography, and frontal facial video data. The primary objective is to develop a trustworthy and robust approach for recognizing mixed emotions by employing advanced signal preprocessing, feature extraction, deep learning, and Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) techniques while systematically addressing the challenges found in the existing approaches to MER. A two-layer Long Short-Term Memory model with feature-level fusion across modalities achieved a final accuracy of 73.02% and an Area Under the Curve score of 85.76% under Leave-One-Subject-Out Cross-Validation. Notably, incorporating multimodal data resulted in an approximate 12.54% improvement in accuracy compared to unimodal approaches. Furthermore, various XAI techniques—such as Test-Time Augmentation and Monte Carlo Dropout for Uncertainty Quantification—demonstrated the model’s trustworthiness and reliability.</p>
</details>

<h3 class="pub-title">Theoretical investigation of a GaAs based novel air slot nano-plasmonic coupler</h3>
<div class="pub-authors">Sumon, M. S. I., Sharif, M. O., <span class="me">Anwar, A. R.</span>, Mahmud, S. S., &amp; Sagor, R. H.</div>
<div class="pub-venue">2020 IEEE Region 10 Symposium (TENSYMP), pp. 1123–1127. IEEE.</div>

<div class="pub-links">
<a class="job-link" href="https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9230972"><i class="fa-solid fa-link"></i> IEEE Xplore</a>
</div>


<details class="pub-abstract">
<summary>Abstract</summary>
<p>A novel rectangular nano-plasmonic coupler based on Gallium Arsenide (GaAs) has been suggested that has an air slot inserted into the dielectric waveguide, considering air as the dielectric material of the metal-dielectric-metal (MDM) plasmonic waveguide. The finite integral technique (FIT) based simulation software yielded a coupling efficiency of 81.07% at the wavelength of optical communication i.e. 1.55μm. The most efficient coupling structure has been attained by analyzing the performance with a variety of different dimensions which subsequently results in decent performance over a wide range of wavelengths above the visible spectrum. The elementary flat rectangular composition of the suggested coupler without any tapered edges provides convenience in the manufacturing process, making the coupler affordable and peerless.</p>
</details>
